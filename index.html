What can I track with THRIVE?

Your symptoms and symptom history, cycle, mood, energy, stress, sleep and lifestyle. You can also log meals, nutrition, water and supplements, and look back at your personal trends.

What’s included in the 14-day trial?

This preview presents the complete experience: tracking, nutrition, trends and insights, reporting, Strength Club, home and gym workouts, yoga, Pilates, mindfulness, sleep and stress support, articles and personalized guidance. Final availability and trial entitlements must be verified before launch.

What happens when my trial ends?

The price, payment requirement and billing start date are awaiting confirmation. Final terms will be shown before a subscription begins. This preview cannot start a trial or take payment.

How do I cancel?

The intended offer is 14 days free, with cancellation before the trial ends. Exact cancellation steps and renewal terms will be confirmed before launch and shown before you subscribe.

Is THRIVE available for iPhone and Android?

THRIVE is designed for iOS and Android, with light and dark app appearances. Store buttons in this preview safely show availability information until the verified THRIVE listings are connected.

Is THRIVE a medical service?

THRIVE is a wellbeing and tracking companion. It does not diagnose conditions or replace care from a qualified healthcare professional. Tracking is a way to keep a personal record of your experience.

Are these genuine customer testimonials?

No. All sample stories are clearly labeled design placeholders. They will be replaced with authentic, permission-based feedback before launch. Illustrative screens and lifestyle images are also identified.
