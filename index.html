Before you begin

A few
good questions.

What is Thrive?

A place for your cycle and everyday wellbeing, together. Track your cycle and symptoms, check in on sleep, energy, mood and stress, log food, water and supplements, and explore Pilates, yoga, strength and mobility in Strength Club.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

We’re planning 14 days free when Thrive launches. The price after the trial, renewal and cancellation terms are still to be announced. You’ll be able to review the full details before starting a subscription.

Can I download it now?

Thrive is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. This is an interactive preview. Downloads, accounts and payments aren’t available here yet.

Do I need to track everything?

Start with what matters to you. A mood check-in, your cycle, a meal or a workout can be a place to begin. You don’t need a perfect streak to keep a record of your days.

Does the app have dark mode?

Yes. The app has light and dark modes. You can try both with the Daily check-in and Nutrition screenshots in the gallery. Cycle and Workouts currently have light screenshots in this preview.
